# Code of Conduct

Everyone participating in the Swiftly project is expected to help make it a
welcoming, respectful, and constructive space.

Examples of expected behavior include:

- Being considerate and open to differing viewpoints.
- Giving feedback that is specific, respectful, and focused on the work.
- Respecting people's privacy and avoiding harassment, discrimination, or
  personal attacks.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and correcting them constructively.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, intimidation, discriminatory
language, unwanted sexual attention, doxxing, or sustained disruption of
project discussions.

Project maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when
necessary to protect the community. To report a concern, contact the repository
owner through the private contact options on
[their GitHub profile](https://github.com/pendekarbau6-create). Do not post
private or identifying details in a public issue.
